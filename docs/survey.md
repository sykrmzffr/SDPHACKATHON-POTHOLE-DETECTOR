# 5-minute resident survey (Google Form)

Send it to family and friends who live in or drive through SDP townships (Elmina, Denai Alam, Bukit Raja, Ara Damansara, Subang Bestari and others) and to class group chats. Even 20–30 responses tonight give you a real number for the evidence criterion (10%) and the impact story.

1. Which township do you live in or drive through most often? (list + Other)
2. How often do you notice potholes or uneven road surfaces there? (Daily / Weekly / Monthly / Rarely)
3. In the last 12 months, has a pothole or rough road caused you any of these? (Tyre/rim/suspension damage · Near-accident · Had to swerve · Slowed down a lot · None)
4. Have you ever reported a road defect? (Yes, to the developer or management · Yes, to the council or MyJalan · No, I didn't know where · No, I didn't think it would be fixed)
5. If you did report one, how long did the repair take? (<1 week · 1–4 weeks · >1 month · Never fixed · Don't know)
6. Would you let a free app on your phone detect potholes automatically while you drive? (Yes / Maybe / No) and why?
7. How important is road quality to your satisfaction with the township? (1–5)

**For the slides:** report "X% notice potholes weekly or more", "Y% have had vehicle damage or a near-miss", and "Z% never reported because they didn't know where or didn't think it would be fixed". If you have no responses before submission, say "survey in progress" rather than inventing numbers.
